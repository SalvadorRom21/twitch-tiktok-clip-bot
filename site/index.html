Twitch Shorts Clip Bot

Local desktop tool for turning Twitch clips and VODs into vertical shorts.

This project helps a creator review, trim, caption, and publish their own short-form clips to platforms like TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram. It runs locally on the creator’s computer and only posts to accounts the creator explicitly connects.

Source code: github.com/SalvadorRom21/twitch-tiktok-clip-bot
