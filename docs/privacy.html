Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

Twitch Shorts Clip Bot (“the App”) is a local desktop tool that helps you turn Twitch clips/VODs into short-form videos and optionally publish them to your own connected social accounts.

1. Who this applies to

This policy covers the App operator’s use of data when you run the App on your own computer and connect third-party accounts (for example TikTok).

2. Data we process

  • Local media: video files, captions, and edit settings stored on your device.
  • Account connection tokens: OAuth access/refresh tokens for platforms you connect (for example TikTok), stored locally on your device.
  • Basic profile identifiers: such as platform user IDs or usernames returned by those APIs to show which account is connected.
  • Twitch content you choose to process: clip/VOD metadata needed to download and edit selected media.

3. How data is used

  • To render, preview, trim, and caption shorts on your machine.
  • To upload/publish content only to accounts you authorize.
  • To keep you signed in to connected platforms until you disconnect.

4. Where data is stored

Media, configs, and OAuth tokens are stored locally under the App’s project folders on your computer (for example data/ and secrets/). The App does not operate a separate cloud account database for end users.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your data. Content and tokens are sent only to the platforms you choose to connect (for example TikTok’s APIs) for authentication and publishing. Those platforms process data under their own policies.

6. Retention and deletion

You can delete local files anytime. Disconnecting a platform in the App removes the stored token file for that platform. You may also revoke access in the platform’s account settings.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: open a GitHub issue.
